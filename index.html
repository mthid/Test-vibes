About me

I turn complex problems into elegant interfaces.

Hi, I'm Alex. I've spent the last six years designing digital products that balance visual polish with real usability. My work spans SaaS dashboards, consumer apps, and full brand identities — always with a focus on clarity, intention, and a healthy obsession with the details others miss.

Before going independent, I was lead designer at a fintech startup in Stockholm where I helped grow the product from 0 to 200k users. Now I work with ambitious teams who want design to be a genuine competitive advantage.
